ZF-Laser

A laser sight for PAYDAY 3

Requires the PAYDAY 3 UE4SS loader:
https://modworkshop.net/mod/47771

Install
  1. Open the loader's Mods folder next to PAYDAY3-Win64-Shipping.exe (PAYDAY3\Binaries\UE4SS\Mods).
  2. Copy the Mods\ZF-Laser folder from this zip into it, so you have
     Mods\ZF-Laser\enabled.txt and Mods\ZF-Laser\Scripts\.
  3. Enjoy

Settings
  In game: Settings > Interface, scroll to the ZF-LASER section.
  Key 0 toggles the laser. Choices are saved to Scripts\settings.lua.
  Defaults and the values without a menu row are in Scripts\config.lua.

Uninstall
  Delete the Mods\ZF-Laser folder.
